02 — Massage

Five ways
to unwind.

Swedish, deep tissue, lymphatic drainage, pregnancy massage and manual body contouring — worked at the pressure that suits you, in a room quiet enough to actually let go.

  • Relieves stress and tension — calms the mind and softens tight muscle.
  • Eases tension headaches — gentle work around the scalp and temples.
  • Supports better sleep — deep relaxation that carries into the evening.

10Cups placed

03 — Cupping therapy

Old practice.
Real release.

Dry cupping uses gentle suction to lift the tissue rather than press into it. Cups are placed along the back and shoulders, then left to work while you lie still and breathe.

  • Targets stubborn tightness across the upper back and shoulders.
  • Complements massage — often booked together as one longer session.
  • A favourite after training or long weeks at a desk.
  • Temporary circular marks are normal and usually fade within days.

04 — Makeup & glam

Skin first.
Then the drama.

Prep, prime and a base matched to your skin in daylight — then whatever the occasion asks for, from a soft everyday glow to a full cut-crease and lash.

  • Matched in natural light so the base never turns grey in photos.
  • Built to last through a long day, a ceremony or a night out.
  • Bridal and bridal-party bookings welcome — get in touch for group timings.
  • Lashes applied to suit your eye shape, not a one-size strip.

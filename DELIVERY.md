# Divine Beauty redesign

A static, responsive editorial website preserving the original name, gold logo, black/gold identity, original imagery, all six service offerings and a testimonial from the supplied site.

## Implemented
- Layered photographic hero with restrained desktop scroll and pointer depth.
- Expandable treatment menu with matching photography and service-prefilled enquiry links.
- Two locally hosted, muted beauty films, offscreen pause, explicit playback controls, poster fallbacks, reduced-motion and Save-Data handling.
- Filtered, horizontally browsable lookbook with accessible native dialogs.
- Chosen look carries into the appointment enquiry; remove or edit it before preparing a message.
- Validated enquiry form, reviewable message, mailto handoff and clipboard fallback. No fabricated successful booking or backend submission.
- Mobile layouts, mobile navigation, keyboard focus styling, skip navigation, dialog focus and Escape handling.

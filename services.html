02Body & wellbeing

Cupping Therapy

Dry cupping places suction cups along the back and shoulders to lift the tissue rather than press into it. It is a traditional practice, popular with anyone who trains hard or spends long days at a desk. The round marks it leaves are normal and usually fade within a few days.

Benefits
Targets stubborn tightness across the upper back
A deep, different sensation to hands-on massage
Pairs naturally with a back and shoulder massage
A favourite for post-training recovery days

Please mention any medication, skin condition or pregnancy when you enquire so Dee can advise whether cupping is suitable for you.
